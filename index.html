Client · Dhruti Charitable Trust · Campaign

Deepavali 2026 Campaign and Helpline Collateral

Dhruti Charitable Trust provides free counselling and operates a mental-health helpline in Chennai. For Deepavali 2026, I developed a greetings campaign for the Trust and its two programmes, Dhruti Saathi and Dhruti Saarathi, pairing seasonal wishes with a brief, supportive message about emotional wellbeing.

Scene imagery was generated with AI to a defined creative brief, including a butterfly formed from light, a paper-cut Chennai skyline and a family celebration. Each layout was then composed and typeset in Illustrator within the Trust's navy and gold identity, with bilingual Tamil and English headlines.

Client · Rikkon Group, LUBERR division

Pre-Sales Enquiry Automation

Rikkon Group is a Chennai-based manufacturer of lubrication systems, engine valves and wire harnesses. Customer enquiries for all its divisions arrived in a single shared inbox and were sorted manually.

I designed and built an automated pre-sales workflow in Make.com. A GPT-4o classifier reads each incoming email, routes it to the appropriate team, matches it to one of nine equipment enquiry forms and issues a branded acknowledgement with a unique enquiry ID. I documented the end-to-end process as a set of engineering-style drawings for review by senior management.
